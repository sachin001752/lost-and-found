🔍 How It Works

Follow these simple steps to use our platform effectively.

👤

1. Create an Account

Register or login to start posting your lost or found items.

📝

2. Post Details

Upload photos and descriptions of the item, and specify the location.

🤝

3. Connect

Use the "Contact User" button to reach out via email or phone.

🎁

4. Return Item

Meet in a safe place to return or collect the item.

🛡️ Safety Tips

Your safety is our top priority. Please follow these guidelines.

  • 📍

    Meet in Public Places

    Always choose a well-lit, busy public location like a cafe or mall for the exchange.

  • 👥

    Bring a Friend

    It's always safer to bring someone along with you during meetings.

  • ⚖️

    Verify Ownership

    Ask the owner for specific details (like a serial number or unique mark) that only they would know.

  • 🔒

    Protect Personal Info

    Never share your home address, bank details, or sensitive IDs on the platform.
