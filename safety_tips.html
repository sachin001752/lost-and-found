🛡️ Safety Tips

Your safety is our top priority. Please follow these guidelines.

📍

Meet in Public Places

Always choose a well-lit, busy public location like a cafe or mall for the exchange.

👥

Bring a Friend

It's always safer to bring someone along with you during meetings.

⚖️

Verify Ownership

Ask the owner for specific details that only they would know to ensure it's their item.

🔒

Protect Personal Info

Never share your home address or sensitive identification documents blindly.
