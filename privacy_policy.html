📄 Privacy Policy

At Lost & Found, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your name, email address, and phone number when you register to facilitate communication between users for recovery of items.

2. How We Use Data

Your contact details are only shared with other users when you post an item, allowing them to contact you regarding the lost/found belongings.

3. Data Security

We implement security measures to ensure your data stays safe and is not accessed by unauthorized third parties.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and related data at any time by contacting our support team.
